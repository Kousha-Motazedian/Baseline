# tools

Dev scripts, e.g. batch-simulating seasons for balance checks.

- `sim-game.ts` (`npm run sim:game -- [seed]`): one game between two generated teams; box scores and the last plays.
- `batch-sim.ts` (`npm run sim:batch -- [--games N] [--seed S] [--balance] [--balance-games N] [--average]`): league averages vs. real pro-league targets (incl. fouls, technicals, foul-outs per game, fast-break points), player-level rotation checks (starter minutes, most minutes, players with 10+ min, team top scorer, 40+/50+ games, rebound/assist leaders; targets from rotations.md section 5), FGA mix by play type, identical-team randomness (margin SD, overtime rate, home edge), and offense/defense balance tests. Teams are realistic league teams by default; `--average` uses uniform, star-free average teams instead.
  - `--league-systems`: every team runs a random archetype system (`generateSystem`) with an average coach on his own system (zero execution edges), to check league-wide drift. Without it no team has coaching, so the targets check the engine exactly as before systems existed.
  - `--systems [--systems-games N]` (default 400): report-only system experiments (see `docs/design/coaches.md`, section 3): perfect vs. poor fit compared with +1 on every attribute, best- vs. worst-fitting archetype, a fitted team vs. a poorly fitted +0.5 team, best vs. worst coach, the familiarity penalty, and each system option's net rating and stat moves. Takes ≈ 4 minutes at 400 games.
  - `--fit-stats`: each system option's raw fit mean and SD over generated league players (the `center` values in `src/core/tactics/fit.ts`).
- `inspect-league.ts` (`npm run inspect:league -- [--seed S] [--leagues N]`): one generated 30-team league: players per CA tier (with mean attribute and two-role count), primary positions, roles held, and the top 15 players. With `--leagues N`, also the mean tier counts, GOATs-per-league frequency and the share of superstars with two roles over N leagues. Use it to check the CA calibration in `docs/design/player-roles.md`.
